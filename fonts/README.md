# Fonts

[Andika](https://software.sil.org/andika/) by SIL International, a typeface designed for children learning to read: its "a" and "g" are the single-story forms they learn to write. Latin subset, regular and bold, from [@fontsource/andika](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@fontsource/andika) 5.3.0. Licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`OFL.txt`).
